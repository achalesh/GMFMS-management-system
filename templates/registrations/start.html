Before you begin

Keep a clear portrait photo ready. You can also attach a Panchayat recommendation, identity proof or supporting certificate when requested. JPG, PNG and PDF documents are accepted.

Your information stays private

Your address, date of birth, equipment and documents are for administration. Public contact sharing is a separate, optional choice.

Application, then review

Submitting an application does not appoint you as a facilitator or issue an official ID. Your application will be verified by the administration.

Progress is saved in this browser session for up to 24 hours. Save your application number after submission. Avoid using a shared device for personal information.
